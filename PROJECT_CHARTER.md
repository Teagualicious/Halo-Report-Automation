# Project Charter

## Working title
Halo Brand-Lift Measurement Case Study

## Business objective
Determine whether campaign exposure was followed by incremental branded-search interest beyond category demand and market-level trends, and assess whether larger normalized media weight was associated with a larger response.

## Primary estimand
The average post-campaign change in the approved branded-search outcome for treated analysis geographies relative to the change in matched low/no-exposure comparison geographies, after accounting for category search demand and approved timing/covariate controls.

## Primary deliverables

- Shared methods and validation guide.
- Primary client/campaign report.
- Replication report or feasibility memo.
- Reusable internal dashboard with a client-safe export mode.
- Reproducibility and handoff package.

## Out of scope unless separately approved

- Individual-level attribution.
- ZIP-level Google Trends lift claims.
- Translation of Trends index points into absolute searches without an external calibration source.
- Sales or revenue attribution from search lift alone.
- Causal impression-dose claims from ordinary observational delivery allocation.
- Automated client publishing before methods and security review.

## Required roles

| Role | Named owner | Core responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor/accountable owner | OPEN | Scope, prioritization, access, release decision |
| Intern/analyst | OPEN | Data preparation, analysis, documentation, outputs |
| Methods reviewer | OPEN | Design, estimator, inference, evidence language |
| Ad operations/data owner | OPEN | Delivery extract, reconciliation, field definitions |
| Account lead | OPEN | Query meaning, event calendar, client context, narrative |
| Dashboard owner | OPEN | Platform, access, data contract, operating support |

## Success criteria

A second analyst can reproduce the estimate from governed inputs; the report and dashboard agree; all claims state geography, period, uncertainty, evidence tier, and limitations; and a weak or invalid case is rejected rather than forced into a positive result.
