# Data Request and Intake Guide

Use the accompanying workbook as the formal intake tracker. Request data for two to four candidate campaigns so the project can select the strongest one or two rather than forcing the first available case.

## Campaign delivery

Required at the finest reliable time and geography grain available, preferably ZIP x week:

- client/campaign IDs and aliases;
- planned and actual flight dates;
- targeted ZIPs/geographies;
- delivered impressions;
- unique reach and frequency, when available;
- spend, GRPs/TRPs, and planned weight, when available;
- tactic/channel/creative placement;
- audience segment;
- pacing status and material campaign changes;
- source-system totals for reconciliation.

## Geography and denominator

- ZIP-to-ZCTA and ZIP/ZCTA-to-analysis-market crosswalk;
- allocation ratio when one ZIP maps to multiple geographies;
- household/population/targetable-universe denominator and release date;
- market serviceability and campaign spillover flags.

## Search measurement

- candidate brand names, spelling variants, product/service names, and ambiguous terms;
- candidate generic category terms;
- candidate negative-control terms;
- approved search type and category filter;
- approved geography and date-window options;
- raw export/API storage and credential/security requirements.

## Confounder and event calendar

- promotions, discounts, launches, store openings/closures;
- PR, sponsorships, earned media, crises, or major news;
- paid search, SEO, social, direct mail, other video, radio, out-of-home, and other campaigns;
- competitor actions and material category events;
- major weather or local disruptions when relevant.

## Validation outcomes

At least one is preferred:

- Search Console branded impressions/clicks;
- direct/organic site sessions or branded landing-page visits;
- calls, lead forms, appointments, store locator use, or directions;
- first-party brand study or survey;
- sales/leads only when timing and geography are sufficiently aligned and interpretation is cautious.

## Data transfer rules

Use approved secure storage. Keep raw extracts immutable. Record filename, source system, owner, extraction timestamp, row count, schema, and checksum in the run manifest. Use client aliases in code and sample data.
