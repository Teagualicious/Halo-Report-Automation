# Halo Client Report Template

## 1. Executive conclusion

State the analysis geography, campaign period, estimated branded-search lift, uncertainty interval, and evidence tier in one paragraph. Do not state a stronger causal conclusion than the approved evidence tier permits.

## 2. What was measured

- Campaign and business objective.
- Actual delivery footprint and normalized weight.
- Approved brand/category query baskets.
- Search geography and time grain.
- Treated and comparison definitions.

## 3. Primary lift chart

Show observed treated outcome, estimated counterfactual or comparison trend, campaign period, and confidence interval. Clearly distinguish observed data from modeled estimates.

## 4. Timing/event-study chart

Show pre-campaign leads and post-launch effects. Annotate material campaign changes and concurrent events.

## 5. Delivery footprint

Map ZIP-level delivered exposure, but label the search-lift analysis at the supported parent geography.

## 6. Impression-weight response

Show predefined exposure tiers and/or a continuous association with intervals and geography counts. Use “associated with” unless stronger identification is approved.

## 7. Validation and robustness

Summarize source reliability, pre-fit, placebo results, alternate queries/controls/windows, leave-one-out influence, spillover review, concurrent events, and secondary outcome comparison.

## 8. Interpretation and business implications

Separate what the analysis supports, what it suggests, and what it cannot establish. Avoid translating relative search interest directly into revenue.

## 9. Methods summary

Provide the simple four-cell explanation, production estimator, uncertainty method, data versions, and evidence tier.

## 10. Limitations and next test

Include aggregate/ecological limitation, Trends normalization/sampling, remaining confounding, delivery-allocation caveat, and a prospective randomized geo-holdout recommendation.
