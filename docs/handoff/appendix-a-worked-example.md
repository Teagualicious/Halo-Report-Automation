# A.1 Illustrative inputs

| **Group**              | **Pre brand index** | **Pre category index** | **Post brand index** | **Post category index** | **Brand/category ratio** |
|------------------------|---------------------|------------------------|----------------------|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Exposed markets**    | 50                  | 100                    | 70                   | 100                     | 0.70                     |
| **Comparison markets** | 50                  | 100                    | 55                   | 100                     | 0.55                     |

Exposed brand/category ratio change = 0.70 / 0.50 = 1.40, or 40% growth. Comparison ratio change = 0.55 / 0.50 = 1.10, or 10% growth.

## A.2 Impression-weight example

| **Weight group** | **Definition**                                    | **Adjusted lift** | **Interpretation**                                         |
|------------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| **No/very low**  | \<100 impressions per 1,000 targetable households | 2%                | Baseline/noise range.                                      |
| **Low**          | 100-249 per 1,000                                 | 9%                | Directional response.                                      |
| **Medium**       | 250-499 per 1,000                                 | 18%               | Larger associated response.                                |
| **High**         | 500+ per 1,000                                    | 24%               | Highest associated response; check selection and interval. |

These values are placeholders only. Final bins must be chosen before viewing the outcome or justified by campaign planning thresholds, and the chart must include uncertainty and market counts.
